## Description  *(≤ 10,000 characters)*

**Dr. Doc is the friendly all-in-one file toolkit that never uploads your files. Free and open source.**

Online converters make you upload private documents to a stranger's server, wait in queues, and hit size limits. Dr. Doc does everything right on your PC, with no account, no internet, no file-size cap, no watermarks and no ads. Drop a file on the little doctor, pick what you want, done.

**CONVERT ANYTHING TO ANYTHING**
• PDF ⇄ Word, plus Word, RTF, ODT, HTML, Markdown and TXT to and from each other
• PowerPoint and Excel → PDF (with perfect fidelity when Microsoft Office is installed)
• Images: JPG, PNG, WebP, AVIF, HEIC (iPhone photos), GIF, BMP, TIFF, ICO, and turn photos into crisp SVG vector art
• Spreadsheets: XLSX, XLS, ODS, CSV, TSV, JSON, XML, Markdown tables and SQL
• Video: MP4, MKV, MOV, WebM, AVI, WMV, GIF ⇄ MP4 and more
• Audio: MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, AAC, OGG, Opus, and extract the sound from any video
• Multi-step conversions happen automatically (for example DOCX → PDF → PNG)

**COMPRESS TO ANY SIZE**
Need a PDF under 2 MB for a form, a photo under 200 KB for a job portal, or a video under 25 MB for email? Type the size you need, or drag a slider to make it 60% smaller. Dr. Doc finds the best quality that fits. It works for photos, PDFs, videos, audio and Word/Excel/PowerPoint files, and shows you the before → after size.

**EDIT PDFs**
Fix a typo in existing text, add text boxes, highlights, shapes, arrows, freehand ink, white-out, images and your signature. Merge, split, reorder, rotate, delete or extract pages; add page numbers and watermarks; add or remove passwords; pull out every image. Your original is never overwritten.

**EDIT DOCUMENTS & SPREADSHEETS**
Open Word documents and Excel spreadsheets, make changes and save them, with no Office subscription needed for everyday edits.

**EDIT VIDEOS**
Trim and split clips, join videos and photos, add music, text and crossfades, change speed, crop, rotate and colour-correct, then export to MP4, WebM, GIF or audio.

**CLEAN UP YOUR PC**
See which drives are filling up, clear temporary files, find duplicate files (compared by content, not just name), track down huge files and empty folders, and empty the Recycle Bin. Everything you remove goes to the Recycle Bin first, so you can always undo.

**PRIVATE BY DESIGN**
• Works 100% offline. No account, no sign-in, no cloud
• Files are read where they are, so even multi-gigabyte videos work
• No telemetry, no tracking, no ads
• Existing files are never overwritten

**MADE TO BE PLEASANT**
A cosy retro-desktop look with a cute mascot, day and night themes, larger-text and reduced-motion options, and a one-minute guided tour. Right-click any supported file in File Explorer and choose **Open with → Dr. Doc**.

*Word, Excel and PowerPoint → PDF use Microsoft Office when it is installed for the most faithful result. Built-in converters are used otherwise.*
