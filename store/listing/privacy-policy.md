# Dr. Doc: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: <!-- DATE --> · Applies to: Dr. Doc for Windows (Microsoft Store)*

**Short version: Dr. Doc does not collect, store, send or sell any personal data. Your files never leave your computer.**

## What Dr. Doc does with your files
Dr. Doc converts, compresses and edits files **entirely on your own PC**. When you add a file, the app reads it from your disk, processes it locally and saves the result to the output folder you choose (by default `Downloads\Dr. Doc`). Files are never uploaded to any server, ours or anyone else's.

Files you drag into the window, and intermediate files created during a conversion, are kept temporarily in the app's local cache folder on your PC. You can delete them at any time with **Clean up → Clear app cache**, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

## Data we collect
None. Dr. Doc has:
* no user accounts or sign-in
* no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising
* no cookies or trackers
* no network communication. The app's window talks only to its own built-in engine over a local connection (`127.0.0.1`) that is not reachable from the internet or your local network.

Your settings (for example theme, output folder and last-used options) are stored only on your PC.

## Microsoft Office integration
If Microsoft Word, Excel or PowerPoint is installed, Dr. Doc can use them on your PC to convert Office documents. This happens locally. Dr. Doc sends nothing to Microsoft. Your use of Office itself is governed by Microsoft's privacy statement.

## Microsoft Store
Microsoft may collect data about app downloads, installs and crashes as part of the Microsoft Store and Windows. That is covered by the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement), not by Dr. Doc.

## Children
Dr. Doc collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.
